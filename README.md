# clanker-scratch

Scratch repository for Clanker Corner Slack and git-host acceptance runs.

## Recipes

### Lasagna

Serves 8. About 45 minutes of prep, 1 hour in the oven.

#### Ingredients

**Meat sauce**

- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 large onion, finely diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 500 g ground beef
- 250 g ground pork
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 800 g crushed tomatoes (2 tins)
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp sugar
- Salt and black pepper

**Béchamel**

- 60 g butter
- 60 g plain flour
- 750 ml whole milk, warmed
- Pinch of nutmeg
- Salt and white pepper

**To assemble**

- 12 dried lasagna sheets (no-boil)
- 250 g mozzarella, grated
- 75 g parmesan, grated

#### Method

1. Heat the olive oil in a wide pan over medium heat. Cook the onion for 8
   minutes until soft, then add the garlic and cook 1 minute more.
2. Raise the heat, add the beef and pork, and brown well, breaking up the
   clumps. Stir in the tomato paste and cook 2 minutes.
3. Add the crushed tomatoes, oregano and sugar. Season, reduce the heat and
   simmer uncovered for 30 minutes, stirring occasionally, until thick.
4. Meanwhile make the béchamel: melt the butter in a saucepan, whisk in the
   flour and cook 2 minutes without colouring. Add the warm milk a splash at a
   time, whisking smooth between additions. Simmer 5 minutes until it coats a
   spoon, then season and add the nutmeg.
5. Heat the oven to 190 °C (375 °F). Spread a thin layer of meat sauce in a
   23 × 33 cm baking dish. Layer: pasta sheets, meat sauce, béchamel, a
   scattering of mozzarella. Repeat for four layers, finishing with béchamel,
   the last of the mozzarella and all the parmesan.
6. Cover with foil and bake 30 minutes. Uncover and bake 20–30 minutes more
   until browned and bubbling at the edges.
7. Rest 20 minutes before cutting — this is what lets the slices hold together.

#### Notes

- Swap the meat sauce for roasted courgette, aubergine and peppers to make it
  vegetarian; salt and drain the vegetables first so the layers do not go watery.
- Assembles up to a day ahead. Refrigerate unbaked and add 10 minutes to the
  covered bake time.

### Spaghetti Bolognese

Serves 4. About 15 minutes of prep, 1 hour of cooking.

#### Ingredients

- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, finely diced
- 1 carrot, finely diced
- 1 celery stalk, finely diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 500 g ground beef
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 400 g crushed tomatoes (1 tin)
- 250 ml beef stock, plus a splash more if needed
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- Salt and black pepper
- 400 g dried spaghetti
- 50 g parmesan, grated, to serve

#### Method

1. Heat the olive oil in a large saucepan over medium heat. Add the onion,
   carrot and celery and cook for 10 minutes until softened. Add the garlic
   and cook for 1 minute more.
2. Raise the heat, add the beef and cook for 5–8 minutes until browned,
   breaking up any clumps. Stir in the tomato paste and cook for 2 minutes.
3. Add the crushed tomatoes, stock and oregano. Bring to a gentle simmer,
   then reduce the heat and cook uncovered for 40 minutes, stirring
   occasionally. Add a splash of stock if the sauce becomes too thick.
   Season with salt and pepper to taste.
4. Near the end of the simmering time, bring a large pot of salted water to
   the boil. Cook the spaghetti according to the packet instructions until
   al dente. Reserve a mug of pasta water, then drain.
5. Toss the spaghetti with the sauce, adding a little reserved pasta water
   as needed to coat the strands evenly. Divide between four bowls and
   serve with the parmesan.

#### Notes

- For a richer sauce, replace half the beef with ground pork.
- For a longer simmer, partially cover the pan and cook the sauce for up to
  90 minutes, stirring occasionally and adding stock as needed.
