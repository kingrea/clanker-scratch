## Quick basil pesto

**Ingredients:** 2 cups fresh basil leaves, 1/3 cup pine nuts, 1/2 cup grated
Parmesan, 1 garlic clove, 1/2 cup olive oil, and salt to taste.

1. Blend the basil, pine nuts, Parmesan, and garlic in a food processor.
2. Slowly add the olive oil while blending until mostly smooth. Season with salt.
3. Toss with hot pasta, adding a splash of pasta water to loosen, or spread on bread.
